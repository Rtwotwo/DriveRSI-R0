# Harness source expansion

The curriculum can opt into `source-expansion-policy.json` under its private experiment directory. The registered policy has source capacities 3 → 5 → 8 → 10. These count distinct sources, not consecutive original visual ranks: existing qualified sources are retained first, then the frozen visual rank supplies additional sources. The decision records original ranks and does not use geography or heading.

Capacity is simultaneous eligible sources, not a lifetime cap: retired/exhausted sources can be replaced within the frozen candidate pool. Native128-sample production and256-update training caps remain. At10 samples/source, ten sources fit; at20 samples/source, at most six are activated even when retrieval capacity is8 or10. Per-source targets are never reduced to make the batch fit.

After at least one generation revision, a settled batch of at least16 fresh outcomes triggers expansion if fresh admitted yield is at most10% or duplicate rejection fraction is at least60%. Carried samples do not count as new yield. Execution uncertainty and visual failures do not trigger this rule. Complete physical/prefetch receipts must reconcile before changing sources. The initial decision is a bounded rule, not evidence of learned optimal thresholds.

Each actual source still needs at least10 distinct admitted samples; enlarging the pool can increase the total target. Cumulative attempt limits remain96 per source in the current campaign. Previously completed branches remain preserved, even when a source becomes budget-exhausted. New sources require the existing asset certificate/reconstruction chain. Known exhausted asset preparation can advance another tier; unknown asset results cannot be replayed. At the final tier or exhausted frozen pool, the controller reports an unresolved problem instead of lowering admission or searching indefinitely.

PDMS remains strictly>.95, source pose PSNR strictly>25 dB. Model, loss, native branch supervision and no-new-caption/feature processing remain unchanged.

The registered carried-cohort accounting recovery verifies all terminal work and rebuilds the derived summary from the sealed current outcomes and previous cohort. It preserves original snapshots and performs no physical/render reexecution. This resolves the observed overwritten accounting-list failure; unrelated failures remain unclassified rather than being described as a storage failure.

The existing private platform displays source capacity, original ranks, selected count, pending asset count and expansion reasons. No new public site or desktop monitor is created.
